LED outputs and timing

Each line has a global first-pixel number, a physical LED count, its own update rate and timing delay. Save the show after editing. Compatible hardware channels are validated before playback.

With built-in audio, use GPIO21 (PCM) and GPIO10 (SPI). PWM pins 18/12 and 13/19 require audio disabled on that Pi. Aliases share hardware channels. Long chains have lower maximum FPS.

Delays range from −10,000 to +10,000 ms. Positive delays keep the tail visible after the song. Negative delays use a pre-roll before the audio starts. Output clocks sample the song timeline rather than slowing it down.

Additional Pis

Install this build on each Pi once. Open its Advanced Users page and copy its pairing key. Pair it here over a trusted wired LAN. Projects and sequence data are transferred before playback; remote Pis follow the master clock and stop if the master heartbeat is lost.

Pairing is for the local network. Keep the Pi web interfaces private. Remote GPIO and multi-Pi timing must be calibrated on the actual hardware; software tests cannot establish physical synchronization accuracy.

Prepare this Pi's hardware

GPIO21 needs no additional preparation in your working setup. GPIO10 requires SPI. PWM preparation disables this Pi's built-in headphone output; use it on a remote Pi that does not play audio.

Preparation saves backups and requires a reboot. Use these controls on the Pi you are configuring. External power, suitable level shifting and fused power injection are still hardware requirements.

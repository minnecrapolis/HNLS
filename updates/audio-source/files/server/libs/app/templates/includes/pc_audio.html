Where is the music coming from?

Both choices play through your selected Pi speaker and drive the lights.

Checking music source…

Pi songs: use the playlist controls below. PC stream: choose songs, pause and skip in Spotify or Pandora on Windows. Stopping reception here does not stop Spotify on your PC. Scheduled show boundaries still apply.

Connect the Windows PC / download sender

Download the PC sender

Extract the ZIP on Windows and double-click Start-HNLS-Audio.cmd. Leave it open, click Stream from PC, then play Spotify or Pandora. Keep Windows volume above zero.

The stream plays live; it is not saved as a recording. The sender captures all sound on the default Windows playback device, including alerts. Both computers must be on the same reachable network. Keep the sender ZIP private: it contains your pairing key. Audio is authenticated but not encrypted. Restart the sender if you change the Windows playback device. Select streaming again after a Pi reboot.
